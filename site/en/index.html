KCP: Course Planner for KMITLSee your whole week before you register.

A browser extension for KMITL students. It turns the course search on the registration site into a drag and drop weekly timetable, merges the repeated rows, flags clashing class and exam times, and keeps your plans in your browser only.

Everything you need to plan a term, in one panel.

Search the three ways the registration site does

Search by curriculum and class year, by subject id, or by subject category. Results arrive as one line course cards that open to show sections, open seats, enrollment conditions and exam dates, with filters for day, credits and full sections.

Drag a section in and see a clash before you drop

As soon as you pick up a section, the timetable shows where each of its meetings will land. A green outline means it fits; red hatching marks a clash with a subject already in your plan, named with the time it overlaps. If it cannot be placed, KCP offers other sections of the same subject that fit. Lecture and practice pairs are always added and removed together.

Exam dates are checked too, not only class times

Two subjects that meet on different days can still share a midterm slot. KCP compares the midterm and final windows of every subject in your plan and will not add a section whose exam clashes. The Summary view lists the term’s exams by date, with your weekly hours and free days.

Built for wide screens, narrow windows and touch

On a laptop the course list sits beside the timetable. On a tablet it opens as a side sheet. In a window narrower than 640 px, the planner shows one pane at a time behind a bottom bar, the timetable reads one day at a time, and a long press picks up a section on a touch screen.

The extension runs on computer browsers that support extensions. See the FAQ about phones.

Share your timetable as a ready to send image

Preview mode lays your plan out as a poster. Pick a template for a 16:9 share, a phone or tablet wallpaper, or an A4 print, then download it, copy the image, or copy it as text for chat apps. The image comes out at the same size whatever screen you export from, and can carry your exam schedule under the timetable.

Templates: Share 16:9, phone wallpaper in portrait and landscape, tablet wallpaper in portrait and landscape, Print A4.

Keep several plans, checked against the latest data every time you open them

Keep several named plans per term, duplicate them, and export them as a file. Each time you open a plan, KCP checks its sections against the latest data. If a time, room or exam date changed, or a section is no longer listed, it shows the old and new values for you to review, and never removes anything on its own.

Your data stays in your browser, and access is read only

KCP has no account, no server and no usage statistics. The extension reads only the public course data the registration site already uses. It never touches your sign in, never fills in or submits forms, and never registers on your behalf.

Get started in three steps

After you install, the registration site’s course search opens with a short welcome, and you can open the planner any time from the Planner button at the bottom right.

  1. 1

    Search for courses

    Search by curriculum and class year, subject id, or subject category.

  2. 2

    Drag sections into the timetable

    Clashing class times and exam times are flagged as you place them.

  3. 3

    Share as an image

    Download or copy your timetable as an image to send to others.

Frequently asked questions

What is KCP?

KCP, or Course Planner for KMITL, is a browser extension for planning your timetable before registration, using the course offerings on the KMITL registration site.

Is KCP an official KMITL system?

No. KCP is an independent tool built by a student. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by King Mongkut’s Institute of Technology Ladkrabang.

Can KCP register for courses for me?

No. KCP is for planning before registration. You still register on the institute’s registration site as usual. The extension never fills in forms or sends requests on your behalf.

Does any of my data leave my computer?

No. Saved plans live in your browser’s storage on this device. There is no account, no server and no usage tracking. Removing the extension removes this data.

Which browsers does it work on?

Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera and other Chromium browsers install it from the Chrome Web Store, and Firefox installs it from Firefox Add-ons, all on a computer. Opera may first need its helper for Chrome Web Store extensions.

Can I use it on my phone?

Not yet. Chrome on phones does not support extensions, and support for Firefox for Android is not yet confirmed. The planner already adapts to narrow windows and touch screens, and the web version in development is planned to work without installing an extension.

When will the web version open?

There is no date yet. When it opens, the Use on the web button on this page becomes a working link. Until then, use the extension on Chrome or Firefox.

How current is the course data?

KCP shows the same data the registration site sends and checks its shape before showing it. Results show when they were fetched, and saved plans are checked against the latest data when you open them. Check against the registration site before you register.

Can I move a plan to another computer?

Yes. Export the plan as a JSON file from the plan menu and import it on the other computer. An imported plan is checked against the latest data the first time you open it.

Does KCP cost anything?

No. KCP is free and open source under the MIT license.
