kcp-display.woff2 is a subset of IBM Plex Sans Thai SemiBold, Copyright 2019 IBM Corp.,
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in OFL.txt next to this file.

The subset keeps Basic Latin, the no-break space, the Thai block and the right single
quotation mark, with every OpenType layout feature so Thai marks stack correctly. As a
modified version it is renamed KCP Display, as the license's Reserved Font Name clause
requires.
