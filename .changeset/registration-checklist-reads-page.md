On the pre registration page the checklist now ticks itself. It reads which of your planned subjects the page lists and with which section, whether the page turned a subject down or said a prerequisite grade is not out yet, the term the page is for, and, after you submit, the result of each subject. Your own mark still overrides what the page shows, and both stay in view. While the page's confirm window is open over a list that differs from your plan, a line above it says how many planned subjects the page holds. KCP reads only these course facts, never your name or student id, keeps them in memory only, and sends nothing.
