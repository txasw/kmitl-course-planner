The plan sheet heading now says what it does, such as Create plan while the create form is open, and the plan button tells a screen reader which plan and term are open. A toast action such as undo can now be reached with the Tab key while the planner is open, and a toast never moves focus when it appears. The English poster dates itself in the Buddhist year, as the Thai one does. Every empty state now offers one action: the course list leads to the search or clears its filters, the selected courses list leads to the courses, a free day reads as one quiet line with a link back to the week, and an empty suggestion ticks every subject or closes. English counts read 1 credit, 1 subject, and 1 course rather than the plural.
