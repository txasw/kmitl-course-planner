A timetable block always shows the subject name. Where a long word does not fit a narrow column, such as the phone week, a suggested plan, or a saved image, the name shows one size smaller and then, for an English name, abbreviated word by word, for example INTR. TO COMP. PROG., instead of the subject code. Short joining words and course numbers such as VIII stay whole. The full name stays in the block details and is what a screen reader announces.
