Exporting and copying no longer go quiet while they work. Download PNG and Copy image show a turning icon and read Creating from the moment they are pressed, Share image reads Sharing, and Copy as text and Copy registration list read Copying, until the usual confirmation appears. The other export actions wait meanwhile, so a second press cannot start another export, and the buttons keep the size they have at rest, so nothing on the toolbar moves.
