The suggested plans dialog is easier to read on a phone. The subjects to fit now sit under a row of group segments, such as In your plan, Curriculum, and GenEd and others, each showing how many of its subjects are ticked, and the GenEd group opens by default so it is clear those subjects can be added. A long list shows its first rows with a control for the rest. A Clear current plan action empties the plan after a warning that names how many subjects leave, and the removal can be undone from the notice. Using a suggested plan now opens the Timetable straight away.
