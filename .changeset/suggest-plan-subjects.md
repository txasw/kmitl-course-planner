The suggestion tick list now names the subjects already in your plan, such as a GenEd added by hand, ticked by default. Untick one to leave it out of the suggested plans, and a ticked one may move to another open section so more subjects fit. The suggest button in the plan status row is now a filled button with its own wand icon, the arrow tooltips of the suggestion carousel open below the arrows so they no longer cover the close button, and the lists on a suggestion slide open with the same chevron as the rest of the panel. On a phone the status row reads No clash beside the green shield instead of showing the shield alone.
