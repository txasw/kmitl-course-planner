A message at the bottom of the planner no longer moves the timetable. The summary row, the timetable, and the phone sharing buttons stay where they are while a message shows and after it leaves. The message now appears just above the summary row, the phone navigation bar, or the phone sharing buttons, and the timetable keeps a little room at its end so its last row can always be scrolled clear of a message. A message also stays above the list of courses with no class time, and a notice about a course that does not fit stays above a message rather than under it.
