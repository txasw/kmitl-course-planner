The course list now uses the same soft cards as the timetable. Course cards and the search chip are white with a light edge and shadow, the sections of an open course sit in one inset panel instead of a box each, and an added course rests on a slightly darker card. Each section has a small Add button at its top right in place of the tall orange strip down its edge. Teacher, condition, and remark lines show two full lines before they fold, and fold only when longer. Every count in the panel uses one dark badge, and the unchanged items in the updates dialog fold behind the same chevron as the rest of the panel.
