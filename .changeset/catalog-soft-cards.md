The course list now uses the same soft cards as the timetable. Course cards and the search chip are white with a light edge and shadow, the sections of an open course sit on one white band with thin rules instead of a box each, and an added course rests on a slightly darker card. Each section has a small Add button at its top right in place of the tall orange strip down its edge. Teacher, condition, and remark lines show two full lines before they fold, and fold only when longer. The counts on the filter button, the bottom bar, the header, and the side rail use one dark badge, and the unchanged items in the updates dialog fold behind the same chevron as the rest of the panel.
