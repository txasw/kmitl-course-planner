On a phone the plan status row keeps to two lines, so the timetable keeps its height. The credits, subjects and unscheduled count lead the first line as icons and numbers, and the clash state, the check status and the suggest button share the second. A credit total outside the usual load shows in warn colour with an alert icon, and the check status shows as one icon; a tap on either reveals the full words, with the check and details actions. In the day view each class is a card that reads from the top: the time range, the subject name in both languages, the room, and the section and lecture or practice at its foot. On the export page of a phone the templates take two compact rows with short names, so the preview poster gets more room, and the line under it names the selected template in full with its size.
