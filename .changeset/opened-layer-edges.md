A tall class detail no longer cuts the status row under it. On a wide screen the detail stands beside its class from under the hour row to the foot of the timetable, so its edges never cross a line of text or an hour label, and a long detail scrolls inside it. The plan name field in create and rename grows to show a long name whole instead of scrolling it out of view. On the export page from 640 px up, the display options open as a panel under the toolbar that moves the templates and the poster down instead of covering their text, and the page scrolls on a short window so the poster keeps its size.
