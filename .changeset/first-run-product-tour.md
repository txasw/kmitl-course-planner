A short tour now walks you through the planner on its real controls: searching for courses, adding a section, reading the timetable, asking for a suggested plan, and exporting. It starts after you choose Start planning in the welcome, and if you installed KCP before this update it shows once the next time you open the panel. Use Back, Next, or Skip at any step, and open it again from the More options menu.
