A missing or clashing class on the timetable now looks like every other class, with its colour edge, name, room, and section chip in their usual places, and marks its state with a red edge and a striped band across its top that no text sits on. On a phone, the suggested plans sheet keeps the option pager and Use this plan pinned at its foot, so you can page and apply from anywhere in the sheet. Paired sections in the exam schedule read "Sections 34 and 35", and English always says clash for classes or exams at the same time.
