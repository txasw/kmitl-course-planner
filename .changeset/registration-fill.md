On the pre registration page the helper can now add a subject to the page's form for you. Press Add for me on the subject in focus, or the plus button on a row of the list, or Add all for me in the card's menu to add every subject still to add in one go, one subject at a time, and KCP enters the subject id, picks your section and practice section in the page's own lists, and presses the page's Add button, while the card shows each step with a Stop button. It stops at the first problem and says why and what to do next, for example a section the page does not offer you, a section listed for more than one curriculum, a clash, or a section with more applicants than seats, which you can still choose to add. When the page says a prerequisite grade is not out yet, the helper first shows you the condition and then presses Acknowledged. KCP never presses Continue or Confirm and never submits your registration: you check the list on the page and submit it yourself. Add for me is on by default, can be turned off in the card's menu, and works only on the version of the page KCP was tested with. When it cannot run, it says why, also when you press it. Hiding the helper, collapsing the card with Escape, or opening the planner during a run stops it, as Stop does, and a run that fails shows Could not add. A touch on the page, scrolling it, or pressing the Checklist button does not: the card stays open with Stop while the run goes on, the list of every subject waits until the run ends, and outside a run only a tap on the page folds the card on a phone, never a scroll. When the page sends a request of its own during a run, as it does when it checks the registration schedule every minute or when you come back to its window, KCP stops to be safe and says so, and pressing Add for me again goes on from where the page left off; Add all for me goes on from the first subject not yet added. A check the page starts when your press brings its window to the front is waited out before KCP begins, so it does not stop the run. When the page closes the registration period while a subject is being added, or reopens it with an empty list, the run stops at once. You need to prepare for registration first, so KCP knows every section of your subjects; on the pre registration page you can do it from the card with Prepare data, and Add for me waits while it reads.
