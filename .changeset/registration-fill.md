On the pre registration page the helper can now add a subject to the page's form for you. Press Add for me on the subject in focus, or the plus button on a row of the list, and KCP enters the subject id, picks your section and practice section in the page's own lists, and presses the page's Add button, while the card shows each step with a Stop button. It stops at the first problem and says why and what to do next, for example a section the page does not offer you, a section listed for more than one curriculum, a clash, or a section with more applicants than seats, which you can still choose to add. When the page says a prerequisite grade is not out yet, the helper first shows you the condition and then presses Acknowledged. KCP never presses Continue or Confirm and never submits your registration: you check the list on the page and submit it yourself. Add for me is on by default, can be turned off in the card's menu, and works only on the version of the page KCP was tested with. When it cannot run, it says why. You need to prepare for registration first, so KCP knows every section of your subjects.
