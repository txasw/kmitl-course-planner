Timetable blocks take the soft card look in the editor and in plan suggestions: a gentle tint of the subject color, a soft edge, and a thin color stripe on the left. Course names show in full whenever the block has room, and the section number moves from the corner to the end of the room line. In the narrow week view on a phone and in suggestion thumbnails, a block shows the course name alone and breaks it between words, so a long Thai name no longer falls apart one letter per line. The shared image keeps its flat colors and gains the fuller names and the new section placement.
