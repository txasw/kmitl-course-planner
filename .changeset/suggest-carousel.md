Suggested plans now show one option at a time, with its week large enough to read in place. Swipe across the option, use the previous and next buttons, tap a dot, or press the left and right arrow keys to see the others; the position, such as Option 2 of 3, is announced as it changes. Each option lists the subjects it places with their full names, and the separate full size view is gone.
