English course names on the timetable, and Thai name fields written in English letters, never break inside a word. In a narrow column, such as the phone week or a suggested plan, a name with a word too long for the column shows one size smaller, and the day view shows it in full. Class blocks, the block details, and the hover card now show the course name in the other language as a quieter second line, as the course list does, and a block drops that line when it has no room. On a phone the timetable opens on the day view every time, on today when you have a class today, with the week one tap away. On wider screens the timetable starts right under its tabs and the days fill the free height.
