English course names on the timetable never break inside a word. In a narrow column, such as the phone week or a suggested plan, a name with a word too long for the column shows one size smaller, or as its subject code, and the day view shows it in full. Class blocks, the block details, and the hover card now show the course name in the other language as a quieter second line, as the course list does, and a block drops that line when it has no room. A missing or clashing class keeps its red stripes behind the text so every line stays easy to read. On a phone the timetable opens on the day view every time, on today when you have a class today, with the week one tap away. On wider screens the timetable starts right under its tabs and the days fill the free height.
