A long plan name now shows in full in the header on phones: the name wraps onto as many lines as it needs and the header grows to hold it, in place of ending in an ellipsis. A plan name is now up to 100 characters, with a live count in the name field, and an imported plan with a longer name is rejected with the reason listed. The export page shows only the chosen template, so no edge of another template with cut text peeks beside it. On a phone the reopened search form keeps Clear search in its pinned action row, so it never sits half hidden at the foot of the form. A folded teacher, condition, or remark line no longer shows a stray Thai mark beside its ellipsis. The switch to another term above the course list, the retry for the search options, the back control of a rejected import, and the controls of the storage notice are all full touch targets on a phone.
