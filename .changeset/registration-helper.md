A registration helper for the pre registration and registration pages. Before registration opens, choose Registration helper in the plan menu and prepare: KCP reads the sections of each subject in your plan once and keeps them for 7 days. On the registration page the Planner button becomes a checklist beside the page, on a phone a sheet at the bottom, listing each planned subject with a copy button for its id, the planned section and its times and exams, the other sections and whether they fit your plan, and any conditions. Mark each subject as you add it on the page, and save a backup section to your plan if you take one. KCP never enters, presses, or submits anything on the registration page; you do that yourself.
