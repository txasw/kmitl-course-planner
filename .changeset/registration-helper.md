A registration helper for the pre registration and registration pages. Before registration opens, choose Registration helper in the plan menu and prepare: KCP reads the sections of each subject in your plan once and keeps them for 7 days. On the registration page a compact card rises above the Checklist button at the bottom right, open on every visit on a wider screen. On a phone, upright or on its side, the page shows first, with the card folded into the Checklist button, which carries the count once the card has read the page's term, until you open it; it then stays as you left it for the rest of your visit. On a screen tall enough the Checklist button sits above the page's own back to top button rather than over it. It shows how many planned subjects the page lists, a row of chips with each full subject id and its status, and the subject in focus with its section in large type, a Copy ID button, and a menu of its other sections that says which fit your plan, so you can pick the section to use or save it to your plan. Press the count for a list of every subject. A subject on the page whose section has printed conditions shows as added with a condition, with the condition in its tooltip, and Check again in the card's menu reads the page afresh. Collapse the card with the Checklist button, or hide it on this page from its menu; it opens again the next time you open the page. The card follows the term the registration page is for, not the plan open in the planner, and names no plan until it has read that term from the page, so a reload never shows a plan of another term while the page loads; when the page term cannot be read, the card says which term its plan is for. The planner's welcome message no longer appears on the registration pages, where it named the planner's plan and covered the card. If you reach the pre registration page without having prepared, press Prepare data in the card: KCP reads your subjects there, one at a time, and Add for me becomes available. The registration page itself still does not prepare. When you have more than one plan for that term, pick the one to follow in the card's menu, and when you have none, the card offers to create one. The helper never switches your plan on its own. Menus and section lists on a phone now respond to a tap, the card and the Checklist button stay on screen when the page is wider than the phone, and messages appear above the card rather than over the Checklist button.
