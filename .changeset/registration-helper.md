A registration helper for the pre registration and registration pages. Before registration opens, choose Registration helper in the plan menu and prepare: KCP reads the sections of each subject in your plan once and keeps them for 7 days. On the registration page a compact card rises above the Checklist button at the bottom right, open on every visit. It shows how many planned subjects the page lists, a row of chips with each full subject id and its status, and the subject in focus with its section in large type, a Copy ID button, and a menu of its other sections that says which fit your plan, so you can pick the section to use or save it to your plan. Press the count for a list of every subject. A subject on the page whose section has printed conditions shows as added with a condition, with the condition in its tooltip, and Check again in the card's menu reads the page afresh. Collapse the card with the Checklist button, or hide it on this page from its menu; it opens again the next time you open the page. The card follows the term the registration page is for, not the plan open in the planner: when you have more than one plan for that term, pick the one to follow in the card's menu, and when you have none, the card offers to create one. The helper never switches your plan on its own. Menus and section lists on a phone now respond to a tap.
