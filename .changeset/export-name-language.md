Exported timetables can name subjects in Thai, English, or both, chosen in the display options and applied the same way to the saved image, the copied image, and the copied text. It follows the panel language until you pick one. A subject code on a short class block now shows whole or not at all, never cut at the edge or split across two lines, and a code the second name pushed out comes back when the second name does not fit. The show English names switch is replaced by the new choice, and names in the online course list wrap instead of being cut short.
