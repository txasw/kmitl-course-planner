The top of the course list is calmer. After a search the form folds into one chip that names the search and opens the form again when tapped, and the time of the data moves into the refresh button's tooltip. The filter, sort and expand controls are icon buttons with tooltips, and the filter icon counts the filters in use. The course count shows once, in the summary line. Each course shows its name in the other language as a quieter second line. A blocked add now replaces the add toast before it, so two notices never stack.
